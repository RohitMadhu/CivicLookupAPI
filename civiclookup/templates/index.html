CivicLookup

Look up U.S. federal and state officials by ZIP or address. Try a request below, or jump straight into the endpoints.

Quick examples

Prefer the native /v1 routes for new apps. Use /api when you need Google Civic-compatible response shapes.

ZIP lookup GET

curl -s '{{ base_url }}v1/lookup/zip/60601' | jq

Address lookup GET

curl -s '{{ base_url }}v1/lookup/address?address=1600+Pennsylvania+Ave+NW,+Washington,+DC+20500' | jq

Include financial disclosures GET

curl -s '{{ base_url }}v1/lookup/zip/66952?include=financial_disclosures' | jq

Google-compatible reps GET

curl -s '{{ base_url }}api/rep/60601' | jq

Useful endpoints

Open any path in your browser or with curl. Full response shapes live in the README.

  • /v1/lookup/zip/<zip> Native ZIP lookup with nested offices and officials
  • /v1/lookup/address Exact district matching via the U.S. Census Geocoder
  • /api/rep/<zip> Google Civic-style representativeInfoResponse
  • /v1/sources/status Bundled data freshness and provenance
  • /health Service health check

Open data from congress-legislators, Open States, and related sources.
